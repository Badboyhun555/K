PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: [Date]

This Privacy Policy describes how K MUSE NOVA ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our website and submit your application for recruitment.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect the following types of information when you submit an application:

  • Personal information: Full name, date of birth, gender, city, state, country, mobile number, and email address.
  • Professional information: Category of talent, experience level, portfolio URLs, social media links, languages, skills, and professional background.
  • Portfolio materials: Photos and videos that you upload as part of your application.
  • Application data: Information about when and how you submitted your application.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To evaluate your application for potential representation or opportunities.
  • To communicate with you about your application status.
  • To verify your identity and the information you provide.
  • To improve our recruitment process and website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

HOW WE USE PORTFOLIO MATERIALS

Photos and videos you submit as part of your application are used solely for the purpose of evaluating your suitability for potential representation or opportunities. These materials are stored securely and are only accessible to authorized personnel involved in the recruitment process.

WHO MAY ACCESS YOUR INFORMATION

Your application information may be accessed by:

  • Authorized K MUSE NOVA personnel involved in the recruitment process.
  • Third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and managing our data, subject to confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law or to protect our rights.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties except as described in this policy.

DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

DATA RETENTION

We retain your application information for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. If your application is not successful, we may retain your information for a reasonable period to consider you for future opportunities, unless you request that we delete it.

YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information.
  • The right to restrict or object to our processing of your personal information.
  • The right to data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.
